Want only your own list? Turn off every built-in type. Privacy Lens will then match only the words, phrases, and regex rules you add below.

Custom regex secret types

Add up to 20 advanced patterns. Each pattern runs locally on text-node slices capped at 4,096 characters. Backreferences, lookbehind, nested repetition, repeated alternation, and multiple unbounded wildcards are rejected to reduce browser lockup risk.

Enter one literal term per line. Matching is case-insensitive and follows normal or camel-case boundaries: “agents” and “AI” both match inside “r/AgentsOfAI,” while lowercase substrings such as “mail” stay untouched.
